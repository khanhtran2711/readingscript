Slide 6

The main website measurement questions

Teaching cue: 23 to 25. Move through the list briskly. This is an overview of the slides that follow, not a second full explanation of every metric.

We can read this list as a sequence of practical questions. Where did people come from? Which content did they view? What did they do during the visit? Did they complete the intended task? Did technical problems make the task difficult?

Sources and channels help organise the acquisition question. Pageviews describe content activity. Engagement, time, and scroll measures provide different kinds of evidence about the visit. Purchase or registration rates connect activity to outcomes. Loading and responsiveness measures help investigate the experience that produced those outcomes.

Although the slide groups these together, they are not all metrics. Source and channel are dimensions. A goal describes an intended result. A KPI is the measure we select to judge progress towards that result.

For UniCart, I would begin with purchase rate and order value, then use device, channel, and technical evidence to investigate the pattern. We do not need to turn every available number into a target. If we target pageviews alone, we could accidentally reward a design that makes people open five pages to find one answer.
