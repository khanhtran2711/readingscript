Google Analytics and certification

Teaching cue: 69 to 72. Point briefly to the older certificate image. Allow 30 seconds for students to identify the two learning environments: Analytics and Skillshop.

This slide shows a certificate and contrasts Universal Analytics with Google Analytics 4. For our current work, GA4 is the platform to learn. Standard Universal Analytics stopped processing new data in 2023, and its broader shutdown began in July 2024. Treat the older version as historical context when reading tutorials. [1]

The certificate image also belongs to the earlier training pathway. It is an example of what course completion evidence looked like at that time. It should not determine which course you enrol in now.

Google’s current Analytics Academy is available through Skillshop. The official help page lists free training courses and a Google Analytics certification. Open the current pathway through that official page, because course organisation and screen layouts can change. [13]

There are two environments to keep separate. Google Analytics is where you work with collected data. Skillshop is where you study the training and take the relevant assessment. Creating access to one does not, by itself, install a tracker on your website.

For your development as analysts, course completion should be accompanied by a practical explanation. If you tell me that a purchase metric is available, I should be able to ask where the event comes from, when it fires, and what you checked. Those questions connect the training to work you can demonstrate.
