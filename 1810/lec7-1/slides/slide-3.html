Probability Density Function

Approximately 6 minutes

This slide introduces the probability density function, or PDF, for the normal distribution.

The supporting notes identify three important elements:

(f(x)), the density,

(\mu), the mean,

(\sigma), the standard deviation.

The notes make the same distinction: a small standard deviation means values are relatively close to the mean, while a larger standard deviation represents greater spread.

Transition

The PDF tells us about density. But very often analysts don't want density—they want an actual probability such as “What proportion of students scored below 19?” For that, we need the cumulative distribution.
