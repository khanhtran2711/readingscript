Slide 3

The Hypothesis-Testing Decision Process

Approximately 4 minutes

Use this slide as a conceptual pause.

Tell students:

Before thinking about formulas, remember this sequence.

We start with a question.

For example:

“Did our advertising campaign increase website traffic?”

Then we define two competing possibilities.

H₀:

The campaign did not increase traffic.

Hₐ:

The campaign increased traffic.

Then we collect a sample.

Then we calculate a statistic.

Then we ask:

If H₀ were true, how unusual would this result be?

And finally:

If the evidence is sufficiently strong:

Reject H₀.

Otherwise:

Fail to reject H₀.

Very important idea

Hypothesis testing is therefore not just:

“calculate p-value.”

It begins before calculation.

The quality of the hypotheses determines whether the test actually answers the research question.

A perfectly calculated p-value for a badly written hypothesis does not give you a good analysis.

Quick classroom check

I say:

“Instagram ads increase website traffic.”

What would a reasonable H₀ say?

Something like:

Instagram ads do not increase website traffic.

And Hₐ?

Instagram ads increase website traffic.

Good.
