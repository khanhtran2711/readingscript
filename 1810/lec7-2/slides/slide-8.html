Treatment and Advertisement Examples

Now move to the analytics example

The slide next introduces:

Is an advertisement campaign effective in increasing website visitors?

This example is much closer to your Data and Web Analytics context.

Suppose before the campaign:

Average weekly visitors = 200.

Then a directional research question might be:

“Does the campaign increase average weekly traffic above 200?”

The hypotheses could be:

[ H_0:\mu <= 200 ]

[ H_a:\mu>200 ]

Why is this right-tailed?

Because we are specifically interested in an:

increase.

This is extremely important

The statistical test should follow the research question.

Do not collect the data first, see which direction looks convenient.

In a well-designed analysis:

research question first,

hypotheses next,

analysis afterwards.
