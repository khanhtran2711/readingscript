Pie Chart

Approximately 4 minutes

a pie chart represents categorical data as slices of a circle, where each slice corresponds to a category's proportion of the whole. It is particularly intended for part-to-whole comparisons.

Suppose This ice cream shop is located in three major cities:LA, San Fra and NewYork

A pie chart can communicate that these cities together form 100% of sales.

If the values do not represent meaningful parts of one whole, a pie chart becomes less appropriate.

When does a pie chart become difficult?

Imagine 15 categories:

9.2%, 8.9%, 8.4%, 8.0%, 7.8%...

Now ask yourself:

Can your eye easily determine whether one slice is 8.4% or 8.9%?

Probably not.

A bar chart would usually support that comparison more clearly.

So, what conclusion do you draw from that? a pie chart can be useful when:

There are relatively few categories.

The categories form one meaningful whole.

The differences are reasonably visible.
