Bar Chart

Approximately 5 minutes

it defines a visualisation for categorical data where rectangular bars have heights or lengths proportional to the values they represent. Vertical bars may also be referred to as column charts.

A bar chart is an excellent choice because humans are quite good at comparing lengths.

We can quickly see:

Which category is highest?

Which is lowest?

How large are the differences?

Why are there spaces between bars?

Ask the class

If the category names are very long, would you prefer vertical or horizontal bars?

Often horizontal bars are easier because the labels have more room.

That is an example of thinking beyond theory.

A good visualisation should make the reader's job easier.

Transition: Another graph can also represent categorical data, but instead of comparing bar lengths, it divides one whole into slices.
