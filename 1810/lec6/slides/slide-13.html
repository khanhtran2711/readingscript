Correlation Matrix

Approximately 6 minutes

This matrix is a way of visualising relationships between multiple variables, often using colour intensity to represent the strength of correlation.

Imagine our dataset contains:

Sales.

Advertising spending.

Website traffic.

Customer satisfaction.

Discount percentage.

Profit.

Instead of creating a separate scatterplot for every possible pair, we can calculate correlations and arrange them into a matrix.

The correlation coefficient normally ranges from:

-1 to +1.

Close to +1 means a strong positive linear relationship.

Close to -1 means a strong negative linear relationship.

Close to 0 means little or no linear relationship.

if there are many variable to analyze.

How many possible pairwise relationships would we need to inspect?

Quite a lot.

A colour-coded correlation matrix allows us to visually scan the relationships.
