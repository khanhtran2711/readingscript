Slide 11

Histogram

Approximately 6 minutes

The slide makes an important distinction: although histograms look similar to bar charts, histograms are designed for continuous numerical data. The horizontal axis contains ranges , while the vertical axis shows frequency.

Imagine I collect the age of 16 people

Looking at 16 numbers is not very informative.

So I group the values:

0–10.

10–20.

20–30.

And so on.

These intervals are called bins.

Now I count how many people fall into each interval.

The resulting graph allows us to see the distribution of age.

This is different from asking:

“Which category is largest?”

Instead, we ask questions like:

Where are most values concentrated?

Is the normal distribution

Is it skewed?

it is useful for understanding the frequency distribution of a continuous variable such as age or income.

And this is especially useful during exploratory data analysis.
