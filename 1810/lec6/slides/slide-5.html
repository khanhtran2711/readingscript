Common Graphs for Data Presentation

Approximately 3 minutes

The PowerPoint lists several common visualisations, including scatterplots, bar charts, histograms, density plots, pie charts, correlation matrices, box plots and line charts.

You do not need to memorise every graph that exists.

There are hundreds.

Instead, you need to understand what analytical problem each one solves.

Today we will concentrate on several of the most useful choices.

As we move through them, keep asking yourself:

What question does this graph answer?

Transition: Our first graph is designed for one of the most important analytical questions: are two numerical variables related?
