Conceptual and Physical Design

Lecture segment

This distinction is particularly important when producing coursework.

Conceptual design asks:

  • What should the user be able to do?
  • What objects exist?
  • How does the interaction work?
  • How will users understand the application?

Physical design asks:

  • What does the screen look like?
  • Where are controls positioned?
  • Which colours and typography are used?
  • What size should elements be?

Students frequently jump immediately into physical design because it is visible and enjoyable.

But attractive screens cannot rescue a fundamentally confusing conceptual model.
