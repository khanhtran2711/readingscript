What is a Conceptual Model?

Lecture segment

Think about opening a door.

Before touching the door, you already possess expectations:

  • handles are normally pulled;
  • flat plates are normally pushed;
  • turning a knob may release a latch.

Those expectations are part of your mental model.

Digital interfaces work similarly.

When users see:

🛒

they may assume:

“Things I intend to buy are stored here.”

The interface did not need to explain the entire mechanism. Existing knowledge helps the user interpret it.
