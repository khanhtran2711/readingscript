Interaction Design Process

Suppose we design an appointment system for elderly patients.

We initially create a calendar interface.

Usability testing reveals that users don't think in terms of calendar slots. They think:

“I want to see Dr Smith sometime Tuesday morning.”

That evaluation might force us to redesign the interaction around doctor + preferred period, rather than expecting users to navigate a complex calendar.

Evaluation therefore does not simply identify cosmetic defects. It can challenge the conceptual model itself.
