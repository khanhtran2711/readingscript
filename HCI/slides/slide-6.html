Slide 6

Contexts for Interaction Design (2)

Lecture segment

This slide demonstrates why HCI has become increasingly important.

Consider the difference between designing:

  • Spotify,
  • a smartwatch,
  • a hospital infusion system,
  • a vehicle dashboard,
  • a smart thermostat.

All involve interaction, but their contexts are radically different.

With Spotify, a minor operational error can be annoying.

With a medical app, the same error could lead to incorrect treatment.

With an in-vehicle interface, forcing a driver to spend five seconds visually searching for a function can become a safety issue.
