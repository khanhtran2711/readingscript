Exploring

The slide uses a Minecraft recreation of an environment to represent exploratory interaction.

Lecture segment

In exploratory interaction, users navigate through an information space or environment.

Rather than executing one predefined task, users discover information through movement and exploration.

Examples include:

  • Minecraft;
  • Google Maps;
  • virtual museums;
  • VR environments;
  • 3D architectural walkthroughs.

Professional consideration

Exploration is useful when discovery itself matters.

But it is less suitable when users need to perform a critical task quickly.

Imagine requiring a hospital employee to “explore” a virtual environment simply to locate an emergency patient record.

Interesting interaction does not necessarily mean appropriate interaction.
