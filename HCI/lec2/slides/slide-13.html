Examples of Mental-Model Mismatch

Lecture segment

These examples show that mental models are built from previous experience.

If you normally pay after eating at restaurants, entering a restaurant that requires payment first may initially feel strange.

No system is wrong by nature. The real issue is this:

Past experiences shape what people expect. Most software works in similar ways. For example, Android users switching to a new platform will look for navigation buttons in familiar places at first. People used to Microsoft Office will expect similar terms when using a different work tool.

When you design a new system, learn what your users are currently using. Competitor analysis is helpful because competitors have already created interaction habits for users. Therefore, changing these familiar patterns means users have to spend effort learning everything again.

For video games, creating a completely new gameplay style can be a great breakthrough and a big success. However, if it isn't done well and forces players to spend too much time learning, it becomes a very bad experience
