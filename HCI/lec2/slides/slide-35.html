Slide 35

Responding

The slide uses a transport application that alerts users when they should leave or get off.

Lecture segment

This interaction is different because the system initiates the interaction.

The system says, effectively:

“Something has happened; now I need your attention.”

Examples include:

  • calendar reminders;
  • navigation alerts;
  • fraud warnings;
  • low-stock notifications;
  • medication reminders.

Key design problem

When systems initiate interaction, designers must decide:

  • when to interrupt;
  • how urgent the interruption should be;
  • how often to notify;
  • what action the user should take.

Too little notification creates missed information.

Too much notification creates notification fatigue.

Eventually users ignore everything.

Therefore responding interactions require careful prioritisation.
