What Conceptual Models Consist Of

Lecture segment

Let's use an e-commerce application as an example for this one.

Concepts

  • Product
  • Basket
  • Order
  • Payment
  • Customer

Relationships

product is added the basket by the customer

The basket becomes an order.

The order receives payment.

Metaphor

We borrow the physical shopping basket/cart.

Interaction

Users manipulate quantities, instruct the system to remove products, and move through checkout.

Together these form a coherent conceptual model.
