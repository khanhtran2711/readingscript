What is a Metaphor?

The lecture uses Kenneth Burke's description of seeing one thing in terms of another.

Lecture segment

In interface design, metaphors allow users to understand unfamiliar technology through familiar ideas.

For example:

Computer storage is abstract.

But we introduce:

  • files;
  • folders;
  • desktop;
  • trash/recycle bin.

Suddenly the unfamiliar computational world resembles familiar office work.

Why does this matter?

Because learning entirely new systems is cognitively expensive.

Metaphors allow designers to reuse knowledge the user already possesses.
