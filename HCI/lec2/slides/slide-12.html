Slide 12

Conceptual Model and Mental Model

The diagram shows the relationship between the designer, system, and user.

Lecture segment

This is one of the most important slides in the lecture.

The designer has an idea about how the system works.

That understanding is represented through the system.

The user cannot directly read the designer's mind. They only experience what the system presents.

From that experience, the user develops their own mental model.

Therefore we effectively have:

Designer intention → Interface/system → User interpretation

Problems occur when these don't align.

HCI principle

A usability problem is sometimes not a badly positioned button.

It is a difference between the designer's conceptual model and the user's mental model.
