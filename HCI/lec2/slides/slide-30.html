Five Types of Interaction

The lecture introduces:

  • Instructing
  • Conversing
  • Manipulating
  • Exploring
  • Responding

Lecture segment

These interaction types give us different ways to conceptualise the relationship between human and system.

Instead of immediately asking:

“Should this be a button or dropdown?”

Ask:

“What kind of interaction should happen here?”

Users may:

  • give the system commands;
  • hold a conversation;
  • directly manipulate objects;
  • explore an environment;
  • respond to something initiated by the system.

Many modern applications combine several of these.
