Mental Models and Icons

The lecture explains that when users see icons, they unconsciously map the visual representation to things they already know.

Lecture segment

Consider these symbols:

▶ 🔍 🗑️

Most experienced technology users quickly interpret them as:

  • play;
  • search;
  • delete.

The meaning is not contained naturally inside the pixels.

It exists because users have learned the conventions.

Interesting class question

Ask students:

“Why does a floppy-disk icon still mean Save when many younger users have never used a floppy disk?”

This demonstrates that interface conventions can eventually become independent of their original physical objects.

Professional implication

Do not assume an unfamiliar icon will be understood merely because it looks elegant.

When meaning is important, icon + text label may be safer.
